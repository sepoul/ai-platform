# MATH AI assitant

The general idea behind the math ai app is to support math lovers in their learning journey.

## User
The typical user for math ai is someone learning regulary daily and outside of some student context for now but rather as research or hobby. The goal is to focus on the personalized guidance for the user rather than a all in wizard.

## Usecases
One of the strong case for math ai is to allow concept decomposition and visual maps of theorems and propositions, that go beyond pure chat. Another case is that this is a logging and thinking space for a student to track his progress, his learning as well as exercices sessions can be found here. The tool helps also structure, maybe the user write a theorem ir speaks it, the tool generates a LateX equivalent, then slowely create the map of concepts to explore, strongely coupled and interactive with the user.

## Future
The future of this math ai app can also support users interested in learning and working with the theorem prover Lean. Once can imagine a double math / lean language learning in a coupled interesting manner. Lean itself can also be used a knowledge base to help ease concept traveling and traversal.
